Ellery Williamson: Team Canada youth athlete & Cornell volleyball

Ellery talks championship pressure, balancing school with training, mentally resetting after mistakes, and why supportive teammates and coaches made all the difference.

“Trust yourself and stay aggressive on the court—no matter the score or the stakes.”

Read the full interview →

Three ideas from the conversation

01 · Support

High performance and joy can exist together.

A supportive environment made practice Ellery’s favourite part of the day—even while she trained and competed at a demanding level.

02 · Reset

Redirect your energy.

If attacking was not working, Ellery focused on serve-receive or defence. The reset became a new way to contribute.

03 · Pressure

Trust the work you have done.

In high-stakes moments, leaning on teammates and trusting hours of practice helped her keep choosing courage over playing safe.
