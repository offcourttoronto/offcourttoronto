The toolkit

Small tools for
big moments.

Not medical advice or perfect answers. Just athlete-friendly starting points and trusted places to learn more.

01

Pre-game pressure

Name it: “I’m nervous because this matters.” Then choose one controllable focus for your first five minutes.

02

The post-loss spiral

Pause the replay. Eat, hydrate, rest, and speak to yourself like you would speak to a teammate.

Learn more ↗
03

Burnout and overload

Feeling drained is information, not failure. Tell a trusted adult, coach, friend, or support person what is taking the most out of you.

04

Confidence dips

Keep a proof list: skills you built, hard moments you survived, and people who see your effort beyond a result.
