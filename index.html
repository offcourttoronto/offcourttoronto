01 / WHY WE EXIST

There’s more to an athlete than a highlight reel.

We’re anonymous by choice, so the focus stays where it belongs: on the stories, the support, and the community.

03 / THE TOOLKIT

Small things
that can help.

Not perfect answers. Just practical starting points for the moments that feel big.

01 Pre-game pressure +

Name it instead of fighting it. Try: “I’m feeling nervous because this matters to me.” Then pick one controllable focus for the first five minutes.

02 The post-loss spiral +

Give yourself a short reset before analysing the game. Eat, hydrate, rest, and talk to yourself like you would a teammate.

03 Burnout and overload +

Feeling drained is information, not failure. Tell a trusted adult, coach, teammate, or support person what is taking the most out of you.

04 Confidence dips +

Keep a proof list: skills you have built, hard moments you survived, and people who see your effort. Revisit it when one performance starts defining everything.
