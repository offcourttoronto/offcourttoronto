The Huddle · community blog

A place to
feel understood.

The Huddle is where athletes can share the parts of sport that are usually left unsaid—and meet responses rooted in care, not comparison.

How it will work

A safe, athlete-led conversation.

We are building The Huddle thoughtfully. Every community post should be respectful, non-judgmental, and focused on support—not diagnosis, coaching criticism, or pressure to “push through.”

For now, submit a story or question through the Offcourt form. We will curate selected posts and invite supportive athlete perspectives so the space stays safe and genuinely helpful.

Community guidelines

Listen first

Validate before you advise.

“That sounds hard” can matter more than the perfect answer.

Keep it kind

No comparison, shame, or judgment.

Every athlete’s experience of sport is different and worth respecting.

Get real help

Community is not crisis care.

If someone may be unsafe, encourage them to contact a trusted adult or 9-8-8 in Canada.
