Our promise

A thoughtful,
athlete-first space.

Support first: Everything we publish should be respectful, kind, and non-judgmental.

No diagnosis: Offcourt offers peer education and community; not medical advice, therapy, or emergency care.

No shame or pressure: We make room for honest experiences without telling athletes to simply “push through.”

Safety matters: If someone may be unsafe, connect them with a trusted adult or immediate professional support.
