Our why

The person
comes first.

We started Offcourt because pressure is part of sport, but it should not be something athletes have to silently carry.

Our belief

You are a whole person—not a performance.

Competitive sport can bring purpose, friendships, challenge, and joy. It can also bring expectations, comparison, injury, and fear of letting people down. Offcourt is a space to say that out loud.

We are anonymous by choice. The spotlight stays on athletes’ experiences, not us. We are here to make conversations about mental health in sport feel normal, welcoming, and real.
